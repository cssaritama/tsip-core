Evidence-ready market statistics

Everyone shows you the odds.
We show you which odds are real.

TSIP measures how the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100 and Bitcoin move through time (sessions, days, weeks, months, quarters, half-years and years) and price levels (the prior period's high, low, open and close). Every statistic is graded against a control, with its sample size, uncertainty, out-of-sample stability and a correction for the number of questions asked.

How every number is graded

A frequency without a control is not evidence. Each card states what it is compared with, and the grade follows published rules, version .

Edge findings · A to F

An effect measured against a control or a null. Adjusted for multiple testing across the whole report (Benjamini–Hochberg).

  • AStrong. q ≤ 0.05, the most recent 30% of the sample agrees (p ≤ 0.10), at least 200 observations.
  • BModerate. q ≤ 0.10 and both halves of the sample agree.
  • CWeak. Nominally significant (p ≤ 0.05) but does not survive the multiple-testing correction.
  • DInconclusive. No reliable difference from the control.
  • FContradicted. The most recent data points the other way.

Base rates · reliability A to D

How often something happens. No null, but precision and stability matter.

  • A95% interval within ±2.5 pp and earlier vs. recent sample within 3 pp.
  • BWithin ±4 pp and 6 pp.
  • CWithin ±7.5 pp and 10 pp.
  • DWider or less stable.

Controls that change the answer

Random-walk timing baseline
In a random walk the high and low of a period cluster near its start and end. Bars are reordered within each period to measure what timing would look like by chance.
Geometry-matched levels
A close near the top of a range reaches the top first more often for purely mechanical reasons. Control bands of the same width and position isolate what the real levels add.
Same-position analogs
The week, month, quarter, half-year and year in progress are compared only with past periods at the same point and in the same position against the prior range.
Dependence-aware uncertainty
Market days are not independent. Intervals come from a circular block bootstrap over days, weeks, months or longer periods.

Data and provenance

Only derived statistics are published. Raw vendor prices are never redistributed. Every dataset is checksummed and checked for completeness.

Pre-registered hypotheses

Statements fixed in advance, with a fingerprint, and evaluated later on data that did not exist when they were written. A track record of statistics, not of tips.
